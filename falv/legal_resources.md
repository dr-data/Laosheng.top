Hong Kong Legal Resources and Reference Materials ⚖️
================
<base target="_blank">

　　This page provides a curated list of official legal resources, judiciary information, and comprehensive reference materials for Hong Kong. It aims to serve legal professionals, researchers, and students by linking to primary sources, databases, and library collections, including those from the University of Hong Kong (HKU) and Hong Kong Public Libraries.

Primary Legal Sources (法定資料)
------------

1 <b>[Hong Kong e-Legislation (HKeL)](https://www.elegislation.gov.hk)</b>
  The official database of Hong Kong legislation. Provides access to current ordinances and subsidiary legislation in both English and Traditional Chinese.
2 <b>[The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Gazette](https://www.gld.gov.hk/egazette/)</b>
  Published by the Government Logistics Department. Contains legal notices, public announcements, and new ordinances.
3 <b>[Basic Law of the HKSAR](https://www.basiclaw.gov.hk)</b>
  The constitutional document of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

Judiciary & Courts (香港司法機構) 🏛️
------------

1 <b>[Hong Kong Judiciary Official Website](https://www.judiciary.hk)</b>
  Information on court structures, services, and daily cause lists.
2 <b>[Judgments and Legal Reference](https://legalref.judiciary.hk)</b>
  Searchable database for judgments from the Court of Final Appeal, High Court, District Court, and other tribunals.
3 <b>[Court of Final Appeal (CFA)](https://www.hkcfa.hk)</b>
  The highest appellate court in Hong Kong.

Government & Policy (政府及立法會)
----------------

1 <b>[Department of Justice (DoJ)](https://www.doj.gov.hk)</b>
  Responsible for providing legal advice to the government, prosecuting criminal offenses, and drafting legislation.
2 <b>[Legislative Council (LegCo)](https://www.legco.gov.hk)</b>
  The legislature of Hong Kong. Provides records of meetings, Hansard, committee reports, and bills.
3 <b>[Law Reform Commission of Hong Kong](https://www.hkreform.gov.hk)</b>
  Reviews and recommends reforms on various aspects of Hong Kong law.
4 <b>[The Ombudsman, Hong Kong](https://www.ombudsman.hk)</b>
  Investigates complaints about maladministration in the public sector.

Reference Materials & Libraries (參考資料及圖書館) 📚
----------------

Extensive collections for historical and contemporary legal and public records.

1 <b>[University of Hong Kong (HKU) Libraries](https://lib.hku.hk)</b>
  Includes the [HKU Law Library](https://lib.hku.hk/lawlib/), one of the most comprehensive academic law libraries in Asia.
2 <b>[HKU Digital Repository](https://repository.hku.hk)</b>
  Access to theses, old books, historical documents, and academic publications.
3 <b>[Hong Kong Public Libraries (HKPL)](https://www.hkpl.gov.hk)</b>
  The public library network. Provides access to digital collections, local history resources, and physical books.
4 <b>[Multimedia Information System (MMIS) - HKPL](https://mmis.hkpl.gov.hk)</b>
  A digital library system offering old newspapers, government documents, historical photographs, and audio-visual materials.
5 <b>[Hong Kong Public Records Office (PRO)](https://www.grs.gov.hk/en/pro_about_us.html)</b>
  The central repository for the permanent archives of the Hong Kong Government. Accessible via the [Carl Smith Collection](https://www.grs.gov.hk/en/carl_smith_collection.html) and other historical archives.
6 <b>[Hong Kong Legal Information Institute (HKLII)](https://www.hklii.hk)</b>
  A free, independent, non-profit facility providing access to legal information in Hong Kong, including cases, legislation, and law journals.
