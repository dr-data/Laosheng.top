UGC-funded Universities (教資會資助大學)
------------

There are 8 universities funded by the University Grants Committee (UGC) in Hong Kong. They are the premier research and comprehensive universities in the territory.

3 🥇 <b>[The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](http://www.ust.hk)</b> 九龍清水灣⠀[Schools](https://hkust.edu.hk/academics/schools-and-program-offices)⠀[Library](https://library.hkust.edu.hk/)⠀[Admissions](https://join.hkust.edu.hk/)
4 🥇 <b>[The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)](http://www.polyu.edu.hk)</b> 九龍紅磡⠀[Faculties](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/academics/faculties-and-schools/)⠀[Library](https://www.lib.polyu.edu.hk/)⠀[Admissions](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/study/)
