Statutory Bodies & Public Organizations (法定机构及公营机构)
----------------

These organizations are established by statute to perform specific public functions.

1 <b>[Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)](https://www.hkma.gov.hk)</b> 香港金融管理局
  The central banking institution.
2 <b>[Securities and Futures Commission (SFC)](https://www.sfc.hk)</b> 证券及期货事务监察委员会
  Regulates the securities and futures markets.
3 <b>[Hospital Authority (HA)](https://www.ha.org.hk)</b> 医院管理局
  Manages public hospitals and clinics.
4 <b>[Hong Kong Housing Authority (HKHA)](https://www.housingauthority.gov.hk)</b> 香港房屋委员会
  Develops and implements public housing programs.
5 <b>[Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Authority (MPFA)](https://www.mpfa.org.hk)</b> 强制性公积金计划管理局
  Regulates MPF schemes.
6 <b>[Urban Renewal Authority (URA)](https://www.ura.org.hk)</b> 市区重建局
  Undertakes, encourages, promotes, and facilitates urban renewal.
7 <b>[Equal Opportunities Commission (EOC)](https://www.eoc.org.hk)</b> 平等机会委员会
  Implements anti-discrimination legislation.
8 <b>[Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD)](https://www.pcpd.org.hk)</b> 个人资料私隐专员公署
  Enforces the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.
9 <b>[Hong Kong Trade Development Council (HKTDC)](https://www.hktdc.com)</b> 香港贸易发展局
  Promotes, assists, and develops Hong Kong's trade.
10 <b>[Invest Hong Kong (InvestHK)](https://www.investhk.gov.hk)</b> 投资推广署
   Attracts and retains foreign direct investment.
